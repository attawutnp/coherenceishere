02 / The shared foundation

From observation
to understanding.

A common foundation connects spatial information to the objects and events it describes. The design follows a simple path from observation to a considered decision.

01

Observe →

Bring maps, imagery, asset records, and observations into a common spatial context.

02

Detect →

Identify changes, intersections, and patterns through defined analytical methods.

03

Understand →

Inspect the affected places, their relationships, and the evidence behind a result.

04

Decide ↗

Compare scenarios and preserve the reasoning that informs a practical next step.

03 / Our approach

Clarity you can examine.

The interface should make complex information easier to understand while preserving its meaning, provenance, and uncertainty.

Evidence stays visible.

Designed to keep source, observation time, method, and limitations close to every analytical result.

Scenarios preserve the base.

Our design keeps scenario changes separate from source data to support comparison and reproducibility.

Analysis produces the numbers.

Our approach uses deterministic tools for calculations, with AI assistance intended to interpret questions and explain evidence.
